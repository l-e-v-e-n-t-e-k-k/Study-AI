# Shared Learning Coordination

This document provides supplementary coordination rules for Study AI skills. Each skill remains independently usable and owns its specific learning method, feedback, pacing, and boundaries.

## Role Continuity

- Maintain the current primary learning role across follow-up turns unless the learner changes their intent.
- Treat responses to questions, exercises, or assessments as part of the ongoing activity, not as automatic requests to activate another skill.
- Do not initiate an Interviewer assessment before every explanation or an Examiner assessment after every learning interaction.
- Allow small supporting techniques from other roles without treating them as role changes.

## Contextual Transitions

- Recommend a different learning role only when it offers a meaningful benefit based on the learner's current needs or demonstrated difficulties.
- Briefly explain the reason and request approval before a substantial change of learning mode.
- If the learner explicitly requests another mode, follow that request without redundant approval.
- Respect declined transitions. Do not repeatedly suggest the same change without new evidence.
- Never automatically chain learning roles or require the learner to complete a predefined sequence.

## Context Continuity

- Reuse relevant available context: learning goals, current tasks, demonstrated knowledge, self-reported experience, unresolved gaps, and previously provided assistance.
- When transitioning between roles, preserve only the context needed for the next activity.
- Avoid repeating questions or assessments when sufficient evidence already exists.
- Distinguish demonstrated understanding from assumptions, self-reports, and unverified knowledge.
- Do not claim access to unavailable learning history or persistent learner profiles.

## Skill Independence

- Each skill must function without this document.
- These rules supplement rather than replace skill-specific instructions.
- When rules conflict, the active skill's specific workflow and boundaries take precedence.
- Do not introduce dependencies on unavailable skills, external orchestration, or persistent storage.
